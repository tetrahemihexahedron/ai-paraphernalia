# Proposed project

## Goals

1. Make it easy for someone to determine how best to take care of their houseplants.

  - where should the plants be put in the house (e.g., based on sun requirements);
  - how should they be potted (soil, size of pot, etc.);
  - how frequently should they be watered;
  - what type of fertilizer should be used and how frequently;
  - how should one check and fix other requirements, like humidity;
  - other care, like repotting or trimming;
  - how to identify signs of stress or disease and what to do;
  - etc.

1. Help take care of the plants.

  For example, the system might help
    - keep the gardener organized and do what is required by, for example, sending watering reminders to their phone;
    - measure and control environmental factors, like soil quality or humidity;
    - diagnose and treat plant disease or signs of stress;
    - etc.

1. Make it easy to find new houseplants that will look great and thrive in the environment.

Note: Systems and processes considered are not required to have all of the specific features listed, which are meant only as examples.

## Implementation constraints

In this initial planning stage, there are no implementation constraints.

A wide range of ideas, including non-standard or unusual approaches, should be considered. Do not reject ideas only because they seem difficult or impossible to implement.
